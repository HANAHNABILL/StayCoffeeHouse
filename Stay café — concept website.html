Our story

Sarah’s idea: a café that isn’t quite a café.

About Stay

We believe a café can feel like a living room: handmade ceramics, flowers on every table and nowhere else to be. Come for a coffee, stay for the chapter.

The reading shelf

Tap a book to see what it says.

Pick a spine.

Stay began with a simple wish: a place where people can read, sit comfortably and take their time. Quiet, cozy, with flowers, greenery and plenty of white light.

The ceramics carry the personality: bronze and speckled-cream glazes, apple green and brick red, hand-painted in plaid, lattice and stripes. Everything else stays calm so the cups can do the talking.

Every piece is made with Ellie Home and customized to Sarah’s taste, in her words from the video caption.
